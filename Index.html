Quick 2-Minute EmailJS Setup Guide
  1. Go to emailjs.com and sign up for a free account.
  2. In Email Services, click Add New Service → choose Gmail → connect muithyaalex2@gmail.com. Copy the Service ID.
  3. In Email Templates, click Create New Template. Set Subject to: {{subject}} and Content to: {{{html_content}}}. Copy the Template ID.
  4. In Account → General, copy your Public Key.
  5. Paste them above and click Save. That's it! Every click sends directly in the background.
